# Noor al-Tajweed (نُورُ التَّجْوِيدِ)
### *Ammi's Quran, Tajweed & Daily Remembrance Companion*

A mobile-first, installable Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for Mum. Built with full Quranic Tashkeel and authentic orthography symbols, English-first guidance and instructions for users who do not understand Arabic, tactile digital Tasbeeh, daily Duas, and a 30-Juz Khatmah tracker. 100% private, offline-ready, and emoji-free.

---

## How to Open the App

The application is running locally on your network:

- **On your computer:** [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)
- **On Mum's phone (connected to home Wi-Fi):** `http://192.168.0.6:8080` (or `http://Azans-MacBook-Air-2.local:8080`)

---

## How to Install Directly onto Mum's Phone

1. **On iPhone (Safari):**
   - Open `http://192.168.0.6:8080` (or `http://Azans-MacBook-Air-2.local:8080`) in Safari.
   - Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom).
   - Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - Tap **Add** in the top right corner. Mum will now have her own app icon on her home screen.

2. **On Android (Google Chrome):**
   - Open `http://192.168.0.6:8080` (or `http://Azans-MacBook-Air-2.local:8080`) in Google Chrome.
   - Tap the **menu button (three dots ⋮)** in the top right.
   - Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
   - Confirm to add it to her home screen.

## Key Features Designed for Mum

### 1. English-First Guidance (No Arabic Required)
- All navigation tabs, dropdown menus, rule titles, and how-to-pronounce guides are written in clear, friendly English with English transliterations so Mum never feels lost.
- Plain-English breakdown of why each rule occurs (e.g., "The Noon Saakin is followed by Hamzah, so pronounce the 'N' cleanly with no humming").
- Urdu translations included across explanations and Duas for additional clarity.

### 2. Full Quranic Tashkeel & Orthography Symbols (رَسْمُ العُثْمَانِيِّ)
- Complete diacritics on all Quranic verses: Sukoon (ْ), Shaddah (ّ), Tanween (ـً ـٍ ـٌ), Fathah, Kasrah, Dammah.
- Authentic Quranic stop signs and orthography symbols:
  - End of Ayah marks (`۝`)
  - Stop signs: `(مـ)` Must stop, `(لا)` Do not stop, `(ج)` Allowed to stop, `(قلى)` Better to stop, `(صلى)` Better to continue, `(ۛ...ۛ)` Embrace stop.
  - Small high Meem for Iqlab (`ـۢ`).
  - Maddah waves (`ٓ`).

### 3. Tactile Digital Tasbeeh Counter
- Sized comfortably for single-hand thumb tapping with gentle acoustic clicks and mobile vibration feedback.
- Preset Prophetic Adhkar with clear English meanings:
  - SubhanAllah (Glory be to Allah)
  - Alhamdulillah (All praise belongs to Allah)
  - Allahu Akbar (Allah is the Greatest)
  - Daily Istighfar, Durood on the Prophet ﷺ, and Kalimah of Tawheed.
- Round completion chime when reaching 33 or 100 counts.

### 4. Tajweed Practice Quiz
- Gentle self-check questions written in plain English.
- Real Quranic verse snippets with immediate validation and clear explanations.
- Restart option with score counter.

### 5. Daily Duas from the Sunnah & Quran
- Daily supplications for Parents, Health & Healing, Ease of Affairs, Peace of Mind, and Quran Knowledge.
- Full Arabic script, English phonetic transliteration, and English/Urdu translations.
- One-tap "Copy Arabic Dua" button.

### 6. 30-Juz Reading Bookmark & Progress Tracker
- Record current Surah, Ayah, and personal notes.
- Interactive 30-Juz checklist: tap any Juz (1 through 30) to mark it complete with a live progress percentage bar.

### 7. Accessibility & Comfort
- **Text Size Scaler (A / A+):** Scaled specifically for Mum's reading comfort without eye strain.
- **Color Themes:** Sacred Emerald and Mushaf Light Parchment modes.
- **Safe Area Support:** Accommodates phone notches and bottom gesture bars.
