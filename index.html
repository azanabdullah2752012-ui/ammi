Install App on Mum's Phone

To install Noor al-Tajweed (نور التجويد) directly on Mum's phone screen like a native app:

Apple iPhone (Safari):
  1. Open this link in Safari on Mum's phone.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top right. Mum will now have her own app icon on her screen!
Android (Chrome):
  1. Open this link in Google Chrome on Mum's phone.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the top right.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm, and the app will install on her home screen.
